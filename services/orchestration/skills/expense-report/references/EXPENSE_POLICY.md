# Contoso Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Policy Owner:** Finance Department  
**Applies to:** All full-time and part-time employees

---

## 1. General Principles

All business expenses must be:
- **Reasonable** — the expense would be considered prudent by a reasonable person
- **Necessary** — directly related to conducting company business
- **Documented** — supported by itemized receipts for amounts ≥ $25

Employees are expected to exercise good judgment and spend company funds as
carefully as they would their own.

## 2. Expense Categories & Limits

### 2.1 Meals & Dining

| Type | Per-Person Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Individual meal (employee only) | $75 | Itemized receipt required |
| Group meal (team) | $50 per person | List of attendees required |
| Client entertainment meal | $150 per person | Client name & business purpose required |
| Coffee / snacks | $15 | No receipt needed under $25 |

- Alcohol is reimbursable up to $30 per person for client entertainment only.
- Tips should not exceed 20%.

### 2.2 Travel — Airfare

| Class | Allowed | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Economy | Always | Default for all flights |
| Premium Economy | Flights > 6 hours | Requires manager pre-approval |
| Business Class | Flights > 10 hours | Requires VP pre-approval |
| First Class | Never | Not reimbursable |

- Book at least 14 days in advance when possible for best rates.
- Preferred booking platform: Contoso Travel Portal.

### 2.3 Travel — Ground Transportation

| Type | Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Rideshare (Uber / Lyft) | $75 per trip | Standard or XL only |
| Rental car | $80 / day (compact) | Insurance coverage included via corporate card |
| Personal vehicle mileage | $0.67 / mile | IRS standard rate for 2025 |
| Parking | $40 / day | Airport long-term or hotel parking |

### 2.4 Lodging

| Location | Nightly Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Domestic — standard cities | $200 | |
| Domestic — high-cost cities (NYC, SF, DC, BOS, LA) | $350 | |
| International | $400 | |

- Hotel bookings should use the Contoso preferred hotel program when available.
- Room service meals count toward meal limits, not lodging.

### 2.5 Office Supplies & Equipment

| Item | Limit | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| General office supplies | $50 per item | Self-approved |
| Software / subscriptions | $100 / month | Manager approval |
| Hardware / equipment | $500 | VP approval required |

### 2.6 Training & Professional Development

| Item | Annual Limit | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| Conferences | $2,500 | Manager approval |
| Online courses / certifications | $1,500 | Manager approval |
| Books / learning materials | $300 | Self-approved |

## 3. Receipt Requirements

- **Required** for any single expense ≥ $25
- Receipts must be **itemized** (not just a credit card slip)
- Digital photos / scans are accepted
- Lost receipt? Submit a Lost Receipt Affidavit (available on HR Portal)

## 4. Submission Deadlines

- Expenses must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of being incurred
- Expenses older than 60 days require VP approval and may be denied
- International travel reports are due within 45 days

## 5. Approval Workflow

| Total Report Amount | Approver |
|---|---|
| Under $500 | Auto-approved |
| $500 – $2,000 | Direct manager |
| $2,000 – $10,000 | VP / Department head |
| Over $10,000 | CFO |

## 6. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are **never** reimbursable:
- Personal entertainment or recreation
- Spouse / partner travel (unless pre-approved for specific events)
- Traffic or parking violations
- Airline upgrades beyond policy limits
- Clothing (unless safety equipment)
- Gym memberships or personal wellness (use the separate wellness benefit)
- Political contributions or donations
- Lost or stolen personal items

## 7. Policy Violations

- First violation: expense returned with explanation; employee may resubmit with corrections
- Repeated violations: manager notified; may affect performance review
- Fraudulent claims: subject to disciplinary action up to termination and legal proceedings
